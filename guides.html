GUIDE

How to build a simple skincare routine

A good routine does not need ten steps. Start with a gentle cleanser if you need one, a moisturizer that suits your skin, and broad-spectrum sunscreen during the day. Add targeted products gradually rather than changing everything at once.

A simple order

  1. Cleanse — especially at the end of the day.
  2. Treat — optional, depending on your goal.
  3. Moisturize — support the skin barrier.
  4. Protect — use sunscreen during the day.

Patch-test new products when appropriate and stop using a product if it causes significant irritation.

BUDGET EDIT

How to shop beauty products on a budget

Set a budget before browsing, compare the price per unit where possible, and prioritize products you will use consistently. A lower-priced product that works for your routine can be more valuable than a premium product you rarely use.

EXPLAINER

How to read a skincare ingredient list

Ingredients are generally listed in descending order by amount, with some regulatory nuances. Look for the purpose of the product, potential irritants relevant to you, and whether the formula fits your routine. Marketing claims alone are not a substitute for understanding the formula.
