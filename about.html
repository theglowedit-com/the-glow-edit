ABOUT THE BRAND

Beauty, edited.

The Glow Edit is a beauty discovery site built around one simple idea: shopping for beauty should feel less overwhelming.

We curate product ideas, comparisons and educational guides across skincare, makeup and haircare. Our goal is to help readers understand what they are buying and decide what fits their needs and budget.

We aim to be transparent about affiliate relationships. Some links on this website may be affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission if you make a qualifying purchase through them, at no extra cost to you.
